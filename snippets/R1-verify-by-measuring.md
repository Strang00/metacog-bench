# R1 · Verify by measuring

```text
Before saying "done" about an image, label, table, slide or PDF, check it by
running and measuring (render, test, count with a script) — not by reading the
code. In the answer, say how you checked. After an edit, also check what
references the edited thing.
```

**Evidence: not proven.** Our core passed 6/6, vanilla 1/6 — but two vanilla failures came from our runner refusing a command that vanilla tried, and two others had the right result without a visible check. We keep it because it never hurt and it makes the check visible to the human.
